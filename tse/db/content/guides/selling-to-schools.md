Schools buy everything from books and uniforms to labs, transport, software and teacher training. Understanding how they buy shortens your sales cycle.

## Who decides
Principals, correspondents/trustees, administrators and heads of department all influence purchases. Large purchases usually need management approval.

## When budgets are set
Most schools plan purchases before the academic year begins (January–April), with smaller top-ups mid-year. Reach schools before plans are finalised.

## What schools look for
- Proof that other schools use you (case studies, references)
- Clear pricing, including bulk and multi-campus pricing
- Reliable delivery, installation and after-sales support
- Compliance: safety standards, data protection for anything that touches student data

## How to stand out
Publish a complete, verified vendor profile; respond to enquiries quickly; run a short webinar that solves a real problem for principals; and offer pilot programmes.
