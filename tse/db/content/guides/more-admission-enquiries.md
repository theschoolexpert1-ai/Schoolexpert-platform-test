Parents now begin their school search online. The schools that win enquiries are easy to find, easy to trust and quick to respond.

## 1. Complete, verified profile
Parents shortlist schools with clear information: boards, grades, fees range, facilities, transport and support for learners. A verified badge tells parents the details have been checked.

## 2. Photos and videos that show daily life
Classrooms in use, labs, sports and events tell parents more than brochures. (Only use photos where parents have consented and children cannot be identified without consent.)

## 3. Respond within one working day
Enquiries go cold quickly. Route leads to a named admissions contact and set alerts on email or WhatsApp.

## 4. Genuine reviews — and replies
Encourage parents to review after they engage with you. Reply to every review professionally; prospective parents read the replies.

## 5. Be present where parents ask questions
Webinars, podcasts and parent events build trust long before admission season.

## 6. Measure what works
Track profile views, enquiries and conversions month by month and adjust.
