Principals read many applications for every vacancy. A focused CV gets you to the interview.

## Lead with a clear headline
"CBSE Mathematics Teacher (Grades 6–10), 7 years" says more than "Dedicated educator".

## Show impact, not just duties
Instead of "Taught Grade 8 science", write "Raised Grade 8 science average from 64% to 78% through weekly lab-based lessons".

## Match the job
Mirror the board, grades and subjects in the job post. Mention relevant training (CBSE workshops, Cambridge, IB) and technology you use in class.

## Keep personal details minimal
Name, phone, email and city are enough. You don't need to include your date of birth, marital status or a photo unless the school asks.

## Two pages maximum
Use short bullet points and consistent dates.
