Every board can produce excellent learners — the right one depends on your child and your plans.

| Board | Approach | Assessment | Often suits |
|---|---|---|---|
| **CBSE** | Structured, nationally uniform syllabus, NCERT textbooks | Board exams in Grades 10 and 12, growing focus on competency-based questions | Families who may relocate within India; students preparing for national entrance exams |
| **ICSE / ISC** | Broad syllabus with strong emphasis on English and detailed subject content | Board exams in Grades 10 and 12; internal assessment and project work | Students who enjoy languages, literature and in-depth study |
| **State Boards** | State-specific syllabus, often with regional-language emphasis | State board exams | Families settled in the state; preparation for state-level entrance exams |
| **IB (PYP, MYP, DP)** | Inquiry-based, international, concept-driven | Continuous assessment plus external exams in the Diploma Programme | Internationally mobile families; students who thrive with independent research |
| **Cambridge IGCSE / A Levels** | Flexible subject choice, international standard | External exams at IGCSE and A Level | Students aiming at international universities; families wanting subject flexibility |

## Questions to ask any school
- Which board(s) and up to which grade?
- How are students supported in the board-exam years?
- What languages are offered and compulsory?
- What results and university or college destinations have recent batches achieved?
