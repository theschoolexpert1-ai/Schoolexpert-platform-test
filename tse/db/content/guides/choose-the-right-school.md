Choosing a school is one of the biggest decisions a family makes. A clear checklist keeps the process calm and comparable.

## 1. Start with your family's non-negotiables
Write down the three things that matter most — for example *distance under 30 minutes*, *CBSE or ICSE*, and *annual fees within budget*. Use them to filter before you look at anything else.

## 2. Board and curriculum
CBSE, ICSE, State Boards, IB and IGCSE differ in pace, assessment and language requirements. Think about likely relocations, competitive exams and your child's learning style. See our guide comparing boards.

## 3. The full cost, not just tuition
Ask for the total annual cost: admission fee, tuition, transport, books and uniforms, activities, excursions and any development charges. Ask how often fees have risen in the last three years.

## 4. Class size and teachers
Ask about the student–teacher ratio, average teacher experience, teacher turnover and how the school trains its teachers.

## 5. Safety and well-being
Check transport safety, CCTV and visitor policies, the presence of a counsellor, the anti-bullying policy and how the school communicates incidents to parents.

## 6. Support for every child
If your child needs learning support, ask whether there is a special educator, how individual education plans work, and what the school does when a child falls behind.

## 7. Beyond academics
Sports, arts, clubs, STEM and life-skills programmes shape a child's week as much as classroom lessons. Ask to see a sample timetable.

## 8. Visit at least two schools
Visit on a normal school day if possible. Watch how teachers speak to children and how children behave between classes.

## 9. Talk to other parents
Verified reviews from parents who have actually engaged with the school are more useful than ratings from strangers.

## 10. Know the admission timeline
Note application windows, interaction or assessment dates, documents required and fee deadlines.
