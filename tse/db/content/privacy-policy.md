## 4. Children's Information – Limited and Service-Specific

The School Expert does not routinely collect children's personal information for general use of the Platform. Children are not required to create accounts or independently submit personal information for ordinary Platform use.

In certain situations, a parent or lawful guardian may specifically request a service relating to a child. We may then collect limited information about the child where reasonably necessary to understand the parent's requirement, identify or contact an appropriate education professional or service provider, arrange a consultation, facilitate the requested service, or follow up on the enquiry.

Depending on the service, information provided by the parent or lawful guardian may include the child's first name or limited identifier, age or age group, gender where relevant, school/class/educational level where relevant, a description of the educational, learning, behavioural, developmental or support requirement, and a brief description of a condition or concern where necessary to respond to the requested service.

Parents and lawful guardians should provide only relevant and necessary information. Unless specifically required, we discourage submission of detailed medical records, diagnostic reports, photographs, identity documents or other highly sensitive information about a child.

By providing child-related information, the parent or lawful guardian represents that they have authority to provide it and request the relevant service. Where consent or another lawful basis is required, we will process the information accordingly.

Child-related information will be used primarily for the specific service or enquiry for which it was provided. We do not sell children's personal information or use it for unrelated marketing purposes.

Where reasonably necessary, limited relevant child information may be shared with an appropriate school, teacher, tutor, special educator, counsellor, consultant or other service provider involved in responding to the parent's request. We seek to limit such disclosure to information reasonably necessary for that purpose.

Parents or lawful guardians may contact us to request correction, withdrawal of consent or deletion of child-related information, subject to applicable law and legitimate retention requirements.
