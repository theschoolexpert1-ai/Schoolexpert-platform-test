# Admin team desks

Staff sign in at `/admin` and see only the desks their role allows (Admin → Privacy & Compliance → Users & staff roles). Admins see everything.

| Desk | Who | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| 🧭 Command Centre | everyone | Plan mix (Basic/Plus/Pro), ARR, renewals due, unpaid registrations, revenue by stream, queues needing attention |
| ✅ Verification Desk | support, verifier | Registration queue with checklists, private-document viewing (audited), listings, tenants, form manager |
| 💳 Subscriptions & Billing Desk | finance, sales | All subscriptions by tier, extend/cancel, complimentary plans, upgrades, sales follow-up list, orders, refunds, plans & pricing |
| 🏦 Payouts Desk | finance | Marketplace seller payouts after the 7-day window (net of tier commission), referral commissions approve/pay |
| 📇 Leads & CRM Desk | sales, support | Leads, support tickets, contact messages |
| 👪 Parent Services Desk | support, pa_agent | Ask The School Expert requests & quotes, Personal Assistance tracking |
| 🛡️ Community & Moderation Desk | moderator, support | Posts, comments, reviews, reports, Parents' Corner, products |
| ✍️ Content Studio | marketing, moderator | Events, podcasts, gallery, newsletters, resources, books, initiatives, FAQs, SEO guides |
| 🎟️ Events & Sponsorship Desk | sales, marketing | Events, registrations, ticket revenue, passes, sponsorships |
| 📈 Growth & SEO Desk | marketing, sales | Funnel, sign-ups/orgs/revenue by UTM source & campaign, landing pages, AI Match demand by city/grade, sponsored-banner approvals, UTM link builder |
| 🔗 Site Integration Desk | marketing | Old www.schoolexpert.in URLs: pages at legacy paths, 301 redirects (single + CSV import/export), embed code |
| 🔐 Privacy & Compliance Desk | admin (DPO) | Data-principal requests (90-day clock), breach register, audit log, staff roles |
| 🩺 Platform Health | admin | Database size, table growth, sessions, CRM/email outbox, migrations, activity |


## Teachers Hub Desk (moderator, support, marketing)
`/admin/teachers-hub` — Hub metrics, mentor applications (approve / reject, teacher is notified), course list with learners and completions, links to edit courses, lessons and tutorials in the Content Studio and workshops in the Events Desk. Private messages are encrypted and never shown to staff.
